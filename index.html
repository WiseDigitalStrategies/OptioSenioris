Introduction

The essence of Optio Senioris

In Roman military history an Optio was a junior officer and second-in-command: the leader’s right-hand man, responsible for making it happen and taking command when the leader was sick. Senioris distinguished a more seasoned, higher-ranking Optio, one shown dignity, deference and respect.

And that is our essence. We advocate for the patient as professionals with a wealth of geriatric care experience when you have reached crisis point and need third-party intervention.

Our belief

The last season of life does not have to be dire

Ailments, infirmity and mobility issues can make the last season of our lives quite challenging. But it does not have to be depressing. It is a journey, and it is our job to support your loved one and help you enjoy every last day together.

We choose to maintain a positive, optimistic attitude, with cheer and resilience.

The Gift of Happiness

Our people

Our staff are all optimists

An optimist generally expects positive outcomes, tends to see the best in people, and keeps a hopeful, sunny outlook even when facing difficult situations. Maintaining that approach is part of the job description of every one of our caregivers.

Often referred to as the “Rolls-Royce” of geriatric caregivers, they are the trusted choice for professional families, prepared to handle any situation in the home or on the go.
