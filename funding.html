Funding

Paying for care: know your options

Paying for care is one of the biggest worries families bring to us. With our financial and situational experience we can navigate you through the support available, point out the pitfalls and how to avoid them, and signpost you to the right professional help.

Medicaid Qualified Income Trust Veterans Grants LTC Insurance

At a glance

Four routes families most often use

Medicaid

Low-cost or free health coverage for eligible low-income families and the elderly or disabled, including nursing home and hospice care.

Qualified Income Trust

Also known as a Miller Trust: a legal tool that lets you qualify for Medicaid long-term care even if your income is over the limit.

Veterans grants

Wartime veterans and surviving spouses aged 65 and over may be entitled to the tax-free Aid and Attendance benefit.

Long-term care insurance

Covers help with activities of daily living, at home or in a facility, which regular health insurance excludes.

Plan ahead

The document every family needs

Funding is only part of the picture. Without a Durable Power of Attorney in place, a spouse or child may have no authority to act when a loved one loses capacity, and guardianship is a slow and costly alternative.
