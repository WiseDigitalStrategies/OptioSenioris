What long-term care insurance covers

Long-term care (LTC) insurance covers assistance with activities of daily living, like bathing or dressing, at home or in facilities, which regular health insurance excludes.

Policies range from traditional stand-alone plans to hybrid life-insurance combinations, typically costing $1,500 to $5,000 a year depending on your age and health.

How coverage works

  • Benefit triggers: benefits usually activate when you need substantial help with at least two Activities of Daily Living (ADLs), or require supervision because of cognitive impairment.
  • Elimination period: a waiting period, typically 30 to 90 days, applies before the policy begins reimbursing your expenses.
  • Florida specifics: Florida participates in the Long-Term Care Partnership Program (LTCPP), which allows policyholders to protect a portion of their assets if they eventually need to apply for Medicaid.

Types of policies

  • Traditional LTC: stand-alone policies that pay a daily or monthly amount over a set period, usually two to five years. Premiums are not always guaranteed and can increase over time.
  • Hybrid (linked-benefit) policies: combine LTC with life insurance or an annuity. You pay a lump sum or fixed payments. If you need care, it pays out; if you pass away without using it, a death benefit is paid to your heirs.

Why consider it

70%Roughly 70% of 65-year-olds will eventually need some form of long-term care

While Medicaid covers these services, you typically have to spend down your assets to qualify. Medicare generally does not cover custodial, long-term care. Purchasing a policy protects your savings and gives you the freedom to choose where you receive care.

Looking into coverage?

It helps to know:

  • Your approximate age
  • Whether you want individual or joint (couple) coverage
  • Whether you prefer a traditional policy or a hybrid life/LTC policy

We can help you work out the right financial path for your needs.

Long-term care insurance providers in Florida

Providers serving Florida residents include:

  • Mutual of Omaha: highly recommended for stand-alone traditional LTC policies.
  • Nationwide: known for policy customization and cash indemnity options.
  • Northwestern Mutual: a top choice for couples seeking joint policies or multi-policy discounts.
  • Bankers Life: offers specialized products such as SimpleChoice Standard, tailored for seniors.
  • Lincoln Financial Group: offers the popular MoneyGuard hybrid policies, combining life insurance with long-term care.
  • OneAmerica: a highly rated option for asset-based (hybrid) policies.

State resources and local guidance

Navigating LTC regulations and comparing rates in Florida can be complex. Consider these authoritative resources for planning:

  • Florida Department of Financial Services: regulates insurance across the state and offers consumer guides through its Long-Term Care Overview.
  • SHINE program: a Florida Department of Elder Affairs initiative that provides free, unbiased counseling on Medicare, Medicaid and LTC insurance.
  • FloridaHealthFinder: use the Long-Term Care Guide to understand state-specific coverage, restrictions and assessment protocols like the CARES program.
