The Optio Seniorist

Our staff are all optimists

An optimist is a person who generally expects positive outcomes, tends to see the best in people, and maintains a hopeful, sunny outlook even when facing difficult situations.

Part of the job description of all of our caregivers is to maintain an optimistic approach. It creates a biological buffer, lowering stress hormones like cortisol and reducing the chronic inflammation that leads to major diseases.

Because of their highly specialized background, our caregivers command top salaries and have access to lifelong employment opportunities.

Mission statement

“Our mission is to raise the standard level of care for assisted living and private duty home care as a leader in the industry.”

We must strive to show our clients respect, dignity and courtesy, and be kind, compassionate caregivers. It is an honor to serve our clients in crisis and our community of seniors, and we should cherish the time we have serving our clients as a team, in harmony and peace with our fellow employees.

Our success

Our success is based entirely on your attitude to work and to those we serve, and on the dedication and commitment you give to your job.

We recognize that our residents have individual needs, and deserve the dignity and respect of having those needs met with professionalism. We accept the challenge of helping each resident maintain the highest level of health, independence, dignity and respect, in an environment that is clean, comfortable, secure, safe and, most of all, happy.

Trust and confidence are the key to success

  • Trust in your product, and stand by your training and experience.
  • Never be late. Reliability is the key for both our families and your employer.
  • Always be five minutes early to a booking. Service delivery is crucial to our success.
  • Teamwork, precision and synchronization.
